THE APPROACH

How TekiDeki tools are built

01 / PRIVATE

Your data stays close.

Where the tool allows it, the work happens in your browser. No account is needed just to get a useful result.

02 / FOCUSED

Less noise. More doing.

A clear purpose, a short learning curve and an interface that gets out of the way. Every detail has a job.

03 / OPEN

Built in the open.

The source is available to read, inspect and improve. Good software should not ask for blind trust.

BEHIND THE TOOLS

One person.
Many useful things.

TekiDeki is the independent project of Kristian Mario Mišković, a developer based in Vršac, Serbia. It is a place for small, well-made tools that earn their place in your bookmarks.

No growth hacks. No feature maze. Just practical software, careful details and a preference for keeping things simple.
